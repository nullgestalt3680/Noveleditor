# Notes on the sample

This file lives in `materials/` and shows up in the Materials pane next to the text.

Put anything here that you write from: research, interview notes, earlier drafts, exported chat logs. Word documents are rendered with headings, bold and tables; PDFs and images are shown as they are.

The materials folder is never sent to the model. If you want the editor to see a passage from here, paste it into the chat.
