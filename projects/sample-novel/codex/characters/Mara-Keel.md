Forty-one. Took over the lighthouse from her uncle after he stopped answering letters. Former surveyor; measures things when she is nervous. Speaks in short sentences and dislikes being asked how she is.

What she wants: to find out why the ledger has entries in a hand that is not her uncle's.
What she fears: that the answer is ordinary.
